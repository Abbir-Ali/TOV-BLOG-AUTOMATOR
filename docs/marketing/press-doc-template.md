# Press Article — Google Doc Template (for the marketing team)

Create one Google Doc per press mention. Use the labels below exactly as written, each on its own line.
Text goes into Shopify word for word.

---

```
CONTENT TYPE: PRESS

OWNER: Cat Dang                          ← becomes the article author

HEADLINE: I Work in Fashion— 33 Chic Amazon Items I'm Eyeing for My Closet, Vanity, and Home
                                          ← the external article's headline, exactly as published

PUBLICATION: Who What Wear

ARTICLE URL: https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/shopping/chic-amazon-fashion-beauty-home-items-2026

COVERAGE DATE: 2026-10-04                (optional)

SUMMARY: A fashion insider's chic Amazon picks for closet, vanity, and home include our Dex Small Round Ottoman.
                                          ← one or two sentences; this is the article body

PRODUCTS:                                 ← one product per line; a TOV product URL is best
Dex Ottoman — https://tovfurniture.com/products/...
Another product — https://tovfurniture.com/products/...

FEATURED IMAGE:                           (needed before publishing)
[paste the image here, on the line below the label]
   — or —
FEATURED IMAGE: https://drive.google.com/...   (a Drive link or image URL on the same line)
FEATURED IMAGE ALT: short description of the image   (optional)
```

---

## Required

OWNER, HEADLINE, PUBLICATION, ARTICLE URL, SUMMARY and at least one product. If any are missing, the tool will
email you to say what needs adding.
