<h2 id="toc1">Plain Glass Just Got Personality</h2>
<p>For a long time, glass decor meant a clear cylinder and a bunch of grocery store tulips. Fine. Forgettable.</p>
<p>Now the glass is the main character. Color is creeping in, shapes are getting sculptural, and even the water glass has a sense of humor. Designers are treating vases like small sculptures instead of filler, and colored glass is gaining ground on clear.</p>
<p>We have been busy. Our newest glass pieces include a set of fish glasses that makes every sip a little more fun, plus a lineup of ruffled, tinted vases in shades from soft pink to amber. Best part: every piece in this edit is under $50.</p>

<h2 id="toc2">Why Glass Works Right Now</h2>
<h3>Color That Plays With Light</h3>
<p>Colored glass does something paint cannot. Light moves through it, so a pink or amber vase by a window glows and throws color across the surface beneath it. Set one near a lamp and it changes with the time of day.</p>
<h3>Sculpture, Not Filler</h3>
<p>Vases have been promoted. A ruffled rim or a pedestal base gives a vase its own silhouette, which means it looks good empty. No flowers required.</p>
<h3>Collected, Not Matched</h3>
<p>Rooms that look gathered over time are winning over rooms that look ordered from a single catalog page. A little whimsy, like a glass with a fish on it, is exactly how that look happens. It tells people you have a sense of humor and good taste at the same time.</p>

<h2 id="toc4">Style It Like You Collected It</h2>
<h3>Group by Color Family</h3>
<p>Cluster two or three vases in related shades. Pink, salmon and peach together look like a deliberate gradient. Seafoam and amber together look like a gallery wall for your console.</p>
<h3>Put It Where the Light Lands</h3>
<p>Colored glass is at its best near a window or beside a lamp. Place a Ruff or a Shell where the sun hits and let the color do the work.</p>
<h3>Let It Stand Alone</h3>
<p>The Ruff is made to look good on its own, so skip the stems if you like. One sculptural vase on a clean surface beats a crowded vignette every time.</p>
<h3>Set the Table With a Wink</h3>
<p>Put the Fishy glasses out at dinner and add one small vase in the center. The table reads as playful and put together instead of themed.</p>
