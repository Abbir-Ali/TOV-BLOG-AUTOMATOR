Clutter Has Met Its Match
Every home has the same villains. The throw blankets that never quite fold. The remote graveyard. The shoes that pile up by the door like they pay rent.
The fix is not a bigger closet. It is a piece of furniture that looks too good to be doing the dirty work. A storage ottoman sits low, looks sharp, and opens up to swallow whatever is making your room feel loud.
We just dropped a fresh round of them, and they are not the sad lidded boxes of your college apartment. Think faux shearling you want to touch, velvet with real presence, and a tray top that turns the whole thing into a surface. Hidden storage, zero compromise on the look.

Where a Storage Ottoman Earns Its Spot
The Entryway
A storage bench is the entryway upgrade nobody thinks of until they have one. Sit to put on shoes, stash the dog leash and the seasonal gloves inside, and let the top hold your bag while you find your keys. The door stops looking like a landing strip.
The Living Room
This is where an ottoman does its most glamorous double duty. Pull it up as a footrest, add a tray and call it a coffee table, or tuck it beside the sofa for extra seating when friends turn up. Inside, the throw blankets and board games disappear.
The Foot of the Bed
A long storage bench at the end of the bed is a styling move designers lean on for a reason. It anchors the bed, gives you somewhere to sit, and hides the extra bedding and out-of-season layers you would rather not see.

Style It Like It Was Never Hiding Anything
Let the Tray Do the Work
Both the Anita and the Bellamy come with a reversible tray top. Flip it to the tray side and you have a styled surface in seconds: a candle, a stack of books, a small vase. Flip it back when you need the seat.
Mix Textures, Not Just Colors
If your sofa is smooth, pick a textured ottoman. Faux shearling against linen, velvet against boucle, chenille against a woven rug. Contrast is what makes a room look collected instead of matched.
Give It One Job on Top
The fastest way to make a storage piece read as storage is to pile things on it. Keep the top edited: one tray, one stack, one object you love. Everything else lives inside, which is the whole point.
Go Long Where the Room Needs an Anchor
A full-length bench like the Thalia or the Poppi does something a small ottoman cannot. It gives the end of a bed or the front of a window a visual stopping point, and it makes the room feel planned.

Frequently Asked Questions
What can I actually store inside a storage ottoman?
Anything soft or flat works best: throw blankets, extra pillows, bedding, board games, kids' toys, shoes and seasonal accessories. Skip anything sharp or heavy that could wear on the upholstery from the inside.
How do I choose between an ottoman, a bench and a stool?
Think about the job. A bench suits an entryway or the foot of a bed where more than one person might sit. An ottoman is the right call in front of a sofa or chair. A stool is the move when floor space is tight and you want something that tucks away.
Can a storage ottoman double as a coffee table?
Yes, and it is one of the best reasons to buy one. Pieces with a reversible tray top, like the Anita and the Bellamy, make that switch easy. Just keep drinks on the tray side and the top stays clear.
Is performance fabric worth it on something people sit on and rest their feet on?
For a piece that gets this much daily contact, yes. Performance fabrics are made to handle everyday wear, which is exactly what a footrest and a bench seat go through. Check each product page for care details.
How do I know a storage bench will fit my space?
Measure the wall or the spot first, then compare it to the product dimensions on the page. If you want a second opinion, TOV's free design service can help you pick the right size and style for the room.
